op macro
Have you ever wondered if @Emre can reach 100 os by having 10/10 macro? Let's find out!

with op macro widget you can sit back and let your constructor and construction turrets shift their task around dynamically according to your needs.

It works best on hybrid wind solar maps like glitter and canis river with a constructor that has a build queue that contain a crap ton of windmills at the start of the queue, then 2 con turret, 1 e storage, more con turrets, then converters, basic and advanced solars. 

The widget will intelligently 🤓 shift the order of the queue around based on your consumption level in order to maximize scaling speed, army production and minimize BP and energy waste.
